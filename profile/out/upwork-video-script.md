## Delivery checklist

- Do not read a full script off the screen. Eyes move and it sounds read. Put the cue words on a sticky note beside the lens.
- Camera at eye level. Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Light in front of you, never behind. Plain wall, tidy room, no clutter.
- Record 10 seconds and listen with headphones before filming the rest. Bad audio loses more viewers than bad video.
- Slow down about 10%. Pause between sections and cut the pauses in the edit.
- Two takes per section, then jump-cut them together. That looks intentional; one long nervous take does not.
- On-screen text: three or four short labels only, for example the stack and "3 client products".
- No music, or barely audible. Many clients watch muted.
- Plain shirt, no logos or slogans.
